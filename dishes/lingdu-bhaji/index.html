Lingdu bhaji

Lingdu bhaji is made with wild fiddlehead fern, a favourite in Himalaya.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make lingdu bhaji?

Fiddleheads boiled, then stir-fried with garlic, cumin and chilli.

Wild ingredients

  • Fiddlehead fern (Diplazium esculentum): in season March to August
